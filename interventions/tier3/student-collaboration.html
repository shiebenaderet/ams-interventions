🤝 Collaboration About Specific Students

⭐ Rating: ★★★★★ 📚 Tier: 3 (Intensive) ⏱️ Time: 30–60 min per meeting 👥 Group Size: Team (3–8 adults)

What It Is

Structured team meetings — usually the Student Study Team (SST) — where multiple adults who know a student (teachers, counselor, admin, specialists, family) gather data, analyze the root cause of a difficulty, and build a coordinated intervention plan.

At AMS: The SST meets weekly on Wednesdays at 10:00 AM. Team: administrators, counselors, school psychologist, FRA, nurse, support para. The counselor is the point of contact for each student. Every core department listed "collaboration about a specific student" as a Tier 3 must-have.

Quality Implementation

✅ What TO Do

  • Come with data, not impressions — attendance, grades, behavior incidents, assessment scores
  • Include the family when possible; their perspective is often the missing piece
  • Identify the root cause before picking an intervention — why is this happening?
  • Assign a specific adult to each action item with a specific date
  • Set the next review meeting before you leave the room

❌ Red Flags

  • Venting session without decisions or action items
  • No follow-up — the plan sits in a binder until the next crisis
  • Decisions made without the people who work with the student every day
  • Family left out of the process, then surprised by the plan

The SST Process

StepWhat Happens
1. ReferAny teacher or staff member refers a student via the MTSS referral process
2. Gather dataTeam collects info from all teachers, specialists, family, student. Academic data, behavior, attendance, life skills.
3. Meet30–60 min meeting to analyze data, identify root causes, build a coordinated Tier 3 plan.
4. ImplementAll team members implement their part of the plan. Clear roles and responsibilities.
5. MonitorProgress monitored weekly or more. Team communicates regularly.
6. AdjustTeam reviews every 2–4 weeks and adjusts as needed.

Before an SST Meeting

  • Gather your specific data — not "he's struggling" but "he's turned in 3 of 12 assignments in the last 3 weeks"
  • Note what you've already tried at Tier 1 and Tier 2, and for how long
  • Come ready with one specific question you need help answering

Monitoring Success

  • Action items from each meeting are completed before the next meeting
  • Student shows measurable improvement in the targeted area within 6–8 weeks
  • All team members (including family) report a consistent understanding of the plan
